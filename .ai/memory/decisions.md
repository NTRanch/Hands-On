# Decisions (Owner rulings)

- DUAL PURPOSE (2026-10-26): Hands-On is (1) the Owner's way
  to learn Rust at the Daddy Style standard, and (2) the
  Owner's evaluation surface for Torogao. Girls, mechanics,
  animations, and sounds graduate to Torogao production only
  on his play verdicts; silence is not a keep, and no record
  may carry a verdict before he plays it.
- DISCIPLINE NAME (2026-10-26): the engineering discipline in
  this lane is **Daddy Style** (SPEC §7.4) — strict
  requirements set by a firm, caring authority, for the
  student's own benefit; rules known in advance, applied
  consistently, never arbitrary; breaking them yields poor
  outcomes mechanically, as consequence, not punishment.
  Public artifacts carry studio attribution only.
- EYE RULE (SPEC §7.2, a law): all girls have heart-shaped
  PUPILS; the iris and the rest of the eye keep her natural
  eye color. The heart is pupil shape only, in every lane,
  every renderer, every state.
- STYLE LANES (SPEC §7.1): House Cel (default) and Pony
  Painterly, selectable per spec; a build does not drift
  between lanes mid-asset. The animation quality bar is the
  motion grammar named in CAST.md — buildup readability,
  spring follow-through, eye behavior, climax choreography —
  and the medium (2D, 3D, sprites) may vary per level while
  the style stays constant.
- REACTION SYSTEM (2026-10-26): every girl carries live sound
  and physical reactions as one coupled system — the buildup
  readable visually and by sound, eyes rolling at climax,
  each physical event drawing a synced vocal reaction whose
  voice peaks with the event and then trails off. One
  fixed-step clock drives eyes, body, voice, and haptics so
  they cannot drift apart.
- AUDIO SILENCE (2026-10-26): no audio testing is ever
  audible unless the Owner expressly presses play himself.
  Generation, QC, and verification are silent — file metrics,
  loudness, waveform and spectrogram only. Nothing autoplays;
  audio reaches the Owner as files only.
- LANE SEPARATION: this game's launcher is never installed
  into the main editor config or any SFW-lane repository.
  Play routes are the per-session `:luafile` and the dedicated
  `NVIM_APPNAME` profile in CURRICULUM.md — nothing persists.
- HAPTICS (fakes only): haptics work in this project runs on
  simulated backends and readouts under the router contract
  (master switch default OFF, ceiling clamped in the router,
  watchdog, synchronous stop). Live hardware is actuated only
  on the Owner's explicit go, in his presence.
- LEVELS ARE STABLE: level ids P1–P9 and S1–S8 and the cast
  assignments in CAST.md do not renumber. New material joins
  as additions, never as silent reordering.
